# Loan.ly 💸

**Decentralized Emergency Loan Networks for Underserved Communities**

## 🚨 Problem

Millions in low-income and rural communities are systematically excluded from formal credit systems due to:
- Lack of credit history or financial documentation
- No access to traditional collateral
- Inaccessible or absent banking infrastructure

When emergencies arise, these individuals often turn to predatory lenders with exploitative terms and high interest rates. Conventional financial institutions, constrained by bureaucracy and lack of transparency, are unable to deliver timely, fair credit.

## ✅ Solution

**Loan.ly** is a decentralized platform designed to directly connect borrowers and lenders, enabling:
- Instant microloans with fair terms
- Social trust-based creditworthiness
- Transparent and fast application processes
- Incentivized repayment through rewards

This empowers underserved communities by making emergency credit **accessible, affordable, and fair**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:**
- Flutter (Dart)
- Android Studio
- UI/UX Design via Figma

**Backend:**
- Serverless functions (edge-deployed)

**Database:**
- PostgreSQL

**Authentication:**
- Supabase

## 🌍 Core Features

- ✅ Decentralized architecture
- 🔁 Repayment tracking with community reward mechanisms
- 💬 Social trust-based microloan eligibility
- 📲 Mobile-first user experience
- 🛡️ Secure and privacy-respecting authentication
